Market Research · Why this works here

A market that nobody can switch off.

Three structural forces make Benin's auto-parts market uniquely durable. None of them depend on GDP growth.

01 / 03
01

Recession-proof demand

When economies contract, people stop buying new cars and keep repairing old ones. The average Benin vehicle is 17 years old, so demand for parts does not follow the economic cycle.

02

Mechanics are the power buyers

Benin's 200,000+ informal mechanics order parts daily. Today they rely on WhatsApp groups with zero price transparency. We give them search, trust and a storefront.

03

The UK advantage

UK-exported vehicles are the source of many cars on Benin roads, and UK sellers hold compatible parts. The supply chain already exists. The marketplace just doesn't.

03

Strategic recommendations

Three moves that determine everything.

The platform succeeds or fails in the first 90 days. These three decisions cannot be deferred.

01

Register the business in Benin first

MTN Mobile Money and Moov Money APIs require a registered Beninese legal entity. Without it, you cannot legally process payments. This is Day 1, not Day 90. Partner with a local lawyer in Cotonou immediately.

02

Mobile Money is the MVP, not a feature

Don't launch with card payments as the primary option. 60% of Benin's digital transactions go through MTN or Moov. Integrate Mobile Money on Day 1, or don't launch.

03

Recruit 100 mechanic sellers before launch

Mechanics buy parts weekly and list inventory constantly. An empty marketplace repels buyers. Spend 30 days pre-launch doing ground-level outreach in Cotonou garages. 100 listings make a credible launch day.

06

Requirements analysis · What must be true

Recommendations, risks and dependencies.

Meeting these requirements depends on a few things going right, and two blockers that can sink the platform if they're ignored.

01

Mobile-first, French-language UI

Build in French from Day 1: all copy, error messages and onboarding. English is secondary. UK sellers need it; local buyers don't.

02

Escrow before ratings

Trust comes from protected transactions, not stars. Launch with MTN/Moov escrow as the core safety net. Ratings can follow in v1.1.

03

Seed with mechanics, not buyers

Supply creates demand here. Recruit 100 Cotonou mechanics as verified sellers before public launch. An empty marketplace repels buyers for good.

04

Phase in UK sellers

UK parts are the differentiated supply. Start with 20–30 vetted UK dealers shipping used OEM parts. Quality over volume in Year 1.

High

Mobile Money API delay

MTN/Moov approval takes 6–10 weeks. If the application isn't filed on Day 1, the platform launches without payments and becomes a classifieds board, not a marketplace.

High

No legal entity in Benin

Mobile Money and commercial contracts both require a registered Beninese entity. This is a blocker, not a risk to manage around.

Medium

Counterfeit parts liability

If a fake part causes a vehicle failure, the platform faces reputational and legal exposure. Define the seller verification policy before launch.

Medium

Low digital literacy

With 28% internet penetration, mechanics need in-person onboarding support. A purely digital sign-up flow will fail with the core seller segment.

Low

Currency volatility (XOF/GBP)

XOF is pegged to the euro, which is stable. UK sellers pricing in GBP still need FX clarity, so set a pricing policy before launch.

External

MTN Bénin and Moov Africa APIs

Every payment feature depends on approved developer access to both networks. There is no workaround. This is a hard dependency.

External

Beninese legal registration

RCCM registration and a BCEAO-compliant local bank account come before any commercial activity. Allow 3–6 weeks with a Cotonou lawyer.

Internal

French content and support

UI strings, push notifications, SMS templates and customer support all need French. Translation must be finished before the development freeze.

Internal

Vehicle compatibility database

Search by vehicle needs data on the makes common in Benin: Toyota, Peugeot, Renault and Mitsubishi dominate. License it or build it.

Partner

UK seller recruitment pipeline

UK supply is the key differentiator. Business development with UK dealers has to start before the platform has inventory to show buyers.
